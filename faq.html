Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about PushDirect — how it works, what we send, and how to manage your subscription.

Getting started
How does PushDirect work? ▼
When you tap "Enable Direct Alerts", your browser asks for permission to send you notifications. Once you allow, PushDirect delivers alerts directly to your device — no app to download, no account to create. Works on Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari on desktop and Android. iOS requires adding the site to your Home Screen first.
Is PushDirect free? ▼
Yes, permanently free for subscribers. PushDirect is funded by the promotion network — brands and retailers pay to have verified deals included in our alert stream. That revenue runs the platform, which means you get a genuinely free, zero-spam service with no hidden costs.
Do I need to create an account? ▼
No account needed for push notifications. One tap enables alerts, one tap stops them. If you want email deal alerts instead, you only need to provide your email address — no password, no profile.
Why can't I see the permission prompt? ▼
If you previously dismissed or blocked notifications for this site, your browser won't show the dialog again automatically. Click the lock icon on the left side of your address bar → Notifications → Allow, then refresh the page and tap Enable again.
Does it work on iPhone? ▼
Yes, on iOS 16.4 or later. You need to add PushDirect to your Home Screen first: tap the Share button in Safari → Add to Home Screen → open the app from your Home Screen → then tap Enable. This is an Apple requirement, not something we can bypass.
Alerts & content
What kinds of alerts will I receive? ▼
Depending on your subscription: flash sales and retail deals, crypto price breakouts and volume spikes, gift card draws, free spin windows, cashback opportunities, insurance comparisons, error fares, and trending market signals. We quality-filter every alert before it ships — if it's not worth your attention, it doesn't go out.
How many alerts will I get per day? ▼
Typically 1–5 per day across all categories. Our goal is zero regret on every push — we'd rather send one genuinely useful alert than five mediocre ones. On quiet days you might get none. That's intentional.
How fast do alerts arrive? ▼
Average delivery is under 1 second from verification to your screen. Crypto signals are delivered within 0.4 seconds of a confirmed market event. Deal alerts are typically 4–9 minutes before the same offer reaches social media or deal aggregator sites.
Are the crypto alerts financial advice? ▼
No. PushDirect delivers market intelligence signals — verified data points about price action, volume, and on-chain activity. We don't recommend trades or investment decisions. All trading decisions are yours alone and made entirely at your own risk.
Are the deals guaranteed to be available? ▼
Deals are delivered in good faith based on information at the time of sending. Flash sales can sell out quickly — that's exactly why we deliver alerts faster than anyone else. PushDirect is not responsible for deals that expire or sell out before you act.
Privacy & data
Do you sell my data? ▼
Never. PushDirect does not sell, rent, or share subscriber data with any third party. Your push notification token is an anonymous browser identifier — it is not linked to your name or email. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.
What data do you collect? ▼
For push subscriptions: an anonymous browser token, your approximate region (country level), browser type, and anonymised engagement data (did you open the notification). We never collect your name, email, or payment details for push subscriptions. For email signups, we collect only what you provide. Full details: Privacy Policy.
Do you use cookies? ▼
We use browser localStorage (not cookies) to store your theme preference and visit count. This data never leaves your device. Google Analytics sets first-party _ga cookies for anonymised analytics. We don't use advertising cookies or third-party tracking cookies.
Managing your subscription
How do I turn off push notifications? ▼
Click the lock icon on the left side of your browser address bar → find Notifications → set to Block. Takes 5 seconds. All PushDirect notifications stop immediately, no confirmation required.
How do I unsubscribe from email alerts? ▼
Every email contains an unsubscribe link at the bottom. Click it and you're removed instantly. Alternatively, reply to any email with "unsubscribe" in the subject line.
Can I subscribe on multiple devices? ▼
Yes. Push notification subscriptions are per-device and per-browser. Enabling alerts on your phone and laptop gives you coverage on both independently. Email subscriptions work across all devices by default.
I subscribed but I'm not receiving alerts. What's wrong? ▼
Check these in order: (1) Notifications are set to Allow in your browser site settings. (2) Your device's system notifications for your browser are enabled — on mobile, check Settings → Notifications → [your browser]. (3) Do Not Disturb mode is off. (4) You haven't blocked the site at the OS level. If all of these are correct and you're still not receiving alerts, try re-enabling by blocking and re-allowing notifications for pushdirect.network.
