Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 June 2026  ·  PushDirect Promotion Network  ·  pushdirect.network

The short version: PushDirect delivers push notifications to your browser. We don't ask for your email, name, or payment details. We don't sell your data. You can unsubscribe in one click at any time.

1. Who we are

PushDirect Promotion Network ("PushDirect", "we", "us") operates the website pushdirect.network and related landing pages. We provide a browser-based push notification service that delivers deal alerts, market signals, and promotional content to subscribers.

2. What data we collect

Because PushDirect operates through browser push notifications, we collect minimal personal data. Specifically:

  • Push notification token — a unique, anonymous identifier generated by your browser when you subscribe. This token is used solely to deliver notifications to your device. It is not linked to your name, email, or any other personal identifier.
  • Subscription metadata — the date and time you subscribed, your browser type, and approximate geographic region (country level only). This is used to improve notification timing and relevance.
  • Interaction data — whether you opened, dismissed, or clicked a notification. This is aggregated and used to improve signal quality. It is not linked to any personal identifier.
  • Analytics data — we use Google Analytics 4 to collect anonymised page view and engagement data, including pages visited, scroll depth, and session duration. IP addresses are anonymised at collection.

If you use the deal signup form at pushdirect.network/deals, you voluntarily provide your email address and optionally your name. This data is processed by Web3Forms and used solely to send you the deal alert emails you requested.

3. What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your name, email address, or phone number for push notification subscriptions
  • We do not collect payment information of any kind
  • We do not track your browsing activity across other websites
  • We do not build individual behavioural profiles
  • We do not use fingerprinting or supercookies

4. How we use your data

We use the data described above to:

  • Deliver push notifications to your subscribed device
  • Measure and improve the quality of our alert signals
  • Understand aggregate usage patterns to improve the service
  • Meet our legal and compliance obligations

We do not use your data for any automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

5. Third-party services

PushDirect uses the following third-party services to operate:

  • RollerAds (push-sdk.com) — push notification delivery infrastructure. RollerAds processes your browser push token to deliver notifications. See rollerads.com/privacy-policy.
  • Google Analytics 4 — anonymised analytics. See policies.google.com/privacy.
  • Web3Forms — email form processing (deals page only). See web3forms.com/privacy.
  • Monetag — promotional content for iOS users who cannot receive web push notifications. See monetag.com/privacy-policy.
  • Cloudflare Pages — hosting and CDN. See cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

6. Cookies and local storage

PushDirect uses browser localStorage (not cookies) to store your theme preference (light/dark) and visit count. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to our servers. We do not use tracking cookies. Google Analytics sets its own first-party cookies under the _ga prefix; these are anonymised and governed by Google's privacy policy.

7. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third party. We do not share your push token or subscription data with advertisers. The promotional content you receive through push notifications is selected by PushDirect based on categories, not individual targeting.

8. Data retention

Push notification tokens are retained for as long as your subscription is active. If you unsubscribe, your token is deregistered from our delivery system. Analytics data is retained for 14 months in accordance with Google Analytics default settings. Email addresses collected via the deals signup form are retained until you unsubscribe from that list.

9. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights including:

  • Access — request a copy of data we hold about you
  • Erasure — request deletion of your data
  • Objection — object to processing of your data
  • Portability — receive your data in a machine-readable format

Because push subscriptions are anonymous tokens with no name or email attached, we cannot identify you from a token alone. To exercise your rights, please contact us and include your browser and device details so we can locate any relevant data.

10. How to unsubscribe

You can withdraw your push notification subscription at any time:

  • Click the lock icon or site settings icon in your browser's address bar
  • Find Notifications and set it to Block
  • No account deletion, no forms, no waiting period

This immediately stops all push notifications from PushDirect on that device.

11. Children's privacy

PushDirect is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has subscribed, please contact us and we will remove the subscription.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy periodically. Material changes will be noted with an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the service after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

13. Contact

For privacy-related enquiries, please email us at privacy@pushdirect.network.
