Last updated: 1 June 2026  ·  PushDirect Promotion Network  ·  pushdirect.network

1. Who We Are

PushDirect Promotion Network ("PushDirect", "we", "us", "our") operates the website located at pushdirect.network and associated sub-pages. We are a promotion and push notification distribution network. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, disclosed, and protected in connection with the Service.

PushDirect is not a data broker, advertising exchange, or subscriber data reseller. Our business model is funded by brands and retailers who pay for verified deal and signal distribution — not by monetising personal subscriber data.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Push Notification Subscription Data

When you enable push notifications, your browser generates a unique push subscription endpoint (a token issued by your browser vendor — Google, Mozilla, Apple, etc.). We receive and store this token to deliver notifications to your device. This token contains no personally identifiable information. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal identifier as part of the push notification subscription process.

2.2 Automatically Collected Technical Data

When you visit the site, our servers and third-party analytics tools may automatically collect:

  • IP address (truncated where required by applicable law)
  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • Referring URL
  • Pages visited and approximate time of visit
  • Device type (mobile / desktop)

This data is used solely for aggregate analytics, abuse prevention, and service improvement. It is not used to identify individual users.

2.3 Email Sign-Up (Deals Page)

If you voluntarily submit your email address via our deals sign-up form at pushdirect.network/deals, that email address is processed by our third-party form provider (Web3Forms). Your email is used only to send deal-related communications you have explicitly requested. We do not sell, rent, or share your email address with third parties for marketing purposes.

2.4 Cookies and Local Storage

We use minimal browser storage (localStorage) for session-state purposes only — for example, recording whether you have already subscribed in order to show the correct UI state. We do not use tracking cookies to build advertising profiles. Third-party scripts on the site (see Section 5) may set their own cookies subject to their own privacy policies.

3. How We Use Information

Information collected is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Delivering push notifications you have consented to receive
  • Analysing aggregate, anonymised usage to improve the Service
  • Detecting and preventing fraud, spam, and abuse
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations
  • Sending deal emails where you have explicitly opted in

We do not use your information for targeted advertising, behavioural profiling, or sale to data brokers under any circumstances.

4. Disclosure of Information

Anonymised analytics data may be retained indefinitely in aggregate form.
