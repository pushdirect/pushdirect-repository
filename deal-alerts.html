The complete guide

What are free deal alerts and how do they work?

Deal alerts are real-time push notifications sent to your browser or device the moment a sale, price drop, or limited offer goes live. Unlike email newsletters that arrive hours later, push notification deal alerts fire in milliseconds — giving you a genuine first-mover advantage before inventory runs out.

PushDirect's deal alert service monitors thousands of retailers, airlines, gaming platforms, and travel sites continuously. Our quality engine scores every deal before it ships — if it doesn't meet the threshold, it doesn't reach you. The result: high-signal alerts with zero filler.

Why push notifications beat email deal alerts

Email deal alerts have a fundamental problem: by the time you open the email, the deal is gone. Flash sales typically sell out in 4–18 minutes. Error fares expire in under 20. Limited gaming drops clear in under 5.

Push notifications arrive on your screen instantly — even when your phone is locked, your browser is closed, or you're in a different app. PushDirect's average delivery speed is 0.4 seconds from verification to your screen. That's the difference between catching a $289 NYC→Tokyo flight and seeing a sold-out error.

Most deal alert services require an email address, an account, and often a paid subscription. PushDirect works differently:

  • No email required — browser push notifications don't need personal information

The best deal alert categories to subscribe to

Different categories have different time windows. Flash retail sales typically last 2–8 hours, giving you enough time to decide. Error fares and gaming drops are the most time-critical — subscribers who act within the first 3 minutes catch 94% of limited drops. Travel deals fall in the middle: 48-hour hotel rates and sale windows are more forgiving but still reward early awareness.

Deal alert FAQ

How many deal alerts will I get per day? ▼
Most subscribers receive 1–5 alerts per day depending on chosen categories. We quality-score every alert — if engagement drops, we send fewer, not more. Our goal is zero regret on every push.
Do deal alerts work on iPhone? ▼
Yes. Android gets full browser push notifications across all major browsers. iPhone users on iOS 16.4+ can enable web push by adding PushDirect to your Home Screen (Share → Add to Home Screen), then launching from there. Takes 30 seconds.
Can I stop deal alerts at any time? ▼
Yes — instantly. Click any notification → browser settings → Notifications → Block. Or click the lock icon in your address bar on pushdirect.network. Takes 5 seconds. No guilt trips, no confirmation loops.
Are the deals exclusive to PushDirect subscribers? ▼
Some are. Retail partners provide early-access windows for PushDirect's subscriber base before deals go public. The average first-mover window is 4–8 minutes ahead of social media and deal aggregator sites.
Is my data safe with PushDirect? ▼
Push notification subscriptions don't require personal data. PushDirect never collects your email, name, or payment information for the free tier. We don't sell subscriber data. Ever.
