Crypto alerts FAQ

How fast are the crypto alerts? ▼
Average delivery is 0.4 seconds from signal verification to your screen. In practice, PushDirect subscribers receive BTC breakout alerts 4–8 minutes before the same event trends on Twitter or Reddit.
Is this financial advice? ▼
No. PushDirect delivers market intelligence signals — verified data points about price action, volume, and on-chain activity. We don't recommend trades. All investment decisions are yours alone.
How many crypto alerts per day? ▼
On average 1–3 crypto alerts per day. Our quality engine is strict — we'd rather send one high-signal alert than five mediocre ones. If market activity is low, you might get zero. That's intentional.
Can I get alerts for specific coins only? ▼
Currently PushDirect sends alerts across our monitored asset universe. Asset-specific filtering is on our roadmap. For now, all crypto subscribers receive signals across BTC, ETH, SOL, and major altcoins.
