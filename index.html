How often will I actually get notifications? ▼
That's entirely up to you. You choose categories and frequency. Most subscribers receive 1–5 alerts per day. We track quality scores on every alert — if engagement drops, we send fewer, not more. Our goal is zero regret on every push.
Can I actually unsubscribe easily? ▼
Yes — and we're proud of this. Click any notification → browser settings → "Notifications" → Block. Takes 5 seconds. No guilt, no confirm loops, no tricks. We'd rather you leave happy than stay resentfully.
Do you sell my data? ▼
Never. PushDirect does not sell or share subscriber data. Your trust isn't a product we monetize.
Does this work on iPhone and Android? ▼
Android: yes, all major browsers. iPhone: add this page to your Home Screen via Share → "Add to Home Screen," then launch from there. iOS 16.4+ supports web push. We walk you through it — it takes 30 seconds.
What makes PushDirect faster than checking the news? ▼
Checking requires you to remember to look. PushDirect monitors thousands of sources continuously and fires the instant a verified threshold is crossed — right to your locked screen. Our average subscriber receives breaking news 4–8 minutes before it trends anywhere.
