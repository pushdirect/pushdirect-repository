Common questions

Quick answers

How does it work? ▼
When you tap "Enable Direct Alerts", your browser asks for permission to send you notifications. Once you allow, PushDirect delivers alerts directly to your device — no app to download, no account to create. Works on Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari.
Do you sell my data? ▼
Never. PushDirect does not sell or share subscriber data. Your trust isn't a product we monetize.
Can I turn it off? ▼
Yes, at any time. Click the site settings icon on the left side of your address bar, find Notifications, and set it to Block. Takes 5 seconds.
Why can't I see the permission prompt? ▼
If you previously dismissed or blocked notifications for this site, your browser may not show the dialog again. Click the site settings icon on the left of your address bar → Notifications → Allow, then refresh the page.

One step to unlock alerts

iOS only allows push notifications from installed apps. Add PushDirect to your Home Screen to enable instant deal alerts — it takes 5 seconds.

How to add:
1. Tap the Share button (□↑) at the bottom of Safari
2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen
3. Tap Add — then open the app and tap Enable

Update iOS to enable push

Push notifications for web apps require iOS 16.4 or later. Go to Settings → General → Software Update to upgrade, then return here to subscribe.
